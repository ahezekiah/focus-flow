# Cap-06: Content Authoring

## Overview
Designer-facing capability for creating the curated themes and playlists users choose from. Themes bundle a visual style with an ambient sound set; playlists bundle an ordered list of songs.

## Business Value
The quality and variety of themes/playlists directly determine how "immersive" the focus experience feels. This capability is what makes the product's themed, layered-audio promise possible.
