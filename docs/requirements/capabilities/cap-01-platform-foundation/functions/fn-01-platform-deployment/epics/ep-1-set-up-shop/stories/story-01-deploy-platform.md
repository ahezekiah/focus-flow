# Story-01: Deploy Platform

## User Story
**As a** Business Owner/Admin,
**I want** to deploy FocusFlow to production with landing, sign-up, login, and core legal pages live
**so that** visitors can reach the app and become users.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Application is deployed and publicly accessible at the FocusFlow URL
- Landing page renders and links to working sign-up and login flows
- Database, auth, and hosting are provisioned and stable
- About, terms, and privacy pages exist and are reachable from the landing page

## Given / When / Then
**Given** the platform has been deployed to production
**When** a visitor navigates to the FocusFlow URL
**Then** they see a fully functioning landing page with working sign-up and login options.
