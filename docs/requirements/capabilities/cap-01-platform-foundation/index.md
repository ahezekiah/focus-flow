# Cap-01: Platform Foundation

## Overview
Establishes the operational foundation of FocusFlow so the platform is deployable, accessible, and legally sound for public use. This capability owns everything a Business Owner or Admin needs to "open the doors" to new users.

## Business Value
Without a live, stable, and compliant platform, no other capability can deliver value. This is the prerequisite for user acquisition.
