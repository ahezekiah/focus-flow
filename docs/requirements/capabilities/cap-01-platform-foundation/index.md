# Cap-01: Platform Foundation

## Overview

Establishes the operational foundation of FocusFlow so the platform is deployable, accessible, and ready to serve visitors on day one. This capability owns everything a Business Owner or Admin needs to "open the doors" — both the technical infrastructure (deployment, hosting) and the seed content (a default theme, default music, and default playlist) that visitors encounter before they've configured anything themselves. Account sign-up and the first-time-user experience are handled by [cap-02-user-onboarding](../cap-02-user-onboarding/index.md).

## Business Value

Without a live, stable platform *and* a working default focus environment, no other capability can deliver value. A visitor who lands on FocusFlow and finds no ambient audio or visual theme available cannot experience what the product is for. This capability is the prerequisite for user acquisition and first-impression quality — and, true to the vision, a visitor can preview the themed, layered-audio experience without being forced to create an account.
